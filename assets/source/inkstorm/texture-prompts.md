# Runtime painted textures

Generated with built-in Codex imagegen. Original outputs copied unchanged into public/assets/inkstorm.

## sky-paint

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production texture asset for a 3D racing game: a seamless 360-degree equirectangular SKY panorama, 2:1 aspect ratio, 2048x1024. Sky only, no terrain, no landscape, no buildings, no text or UI. Warm apricot atmosphere at horizon, muted dusty mauve very top, clusters of small painterly ivory cumulus clouds with lavender underside. Hand painted high-end animated film background, confident but delicate brush detail. Mostly open luminous sky, cloud groups at varied scales and heights, avoid enormous flat blobs or stripes. Left and right edges must match for wrapping. No visible sun disks. This complements the approved Inkstorm racing concept.

## rock-paint

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a tileable square albedo texture atlas of layered eroded desert sandstone, 2048x2048, for a premium painterly 3D racing game. Orthographic flat surface, even diffuse lighting, no perspective, no objects, no large cast shadows. Coral-red, burnt orange and warm sienna strata with small muted indigo mineral fissures; irregular horizontal thin geological seams, chipped edges, subtle brushwork and grit. Rich handpainted stylized material, readable medium-scale rock grain, seamless all four edges. Avoid photorealism, black cavities, diagonal gigantic stripes, text or watermark.


## Ground paint

Original built-in imagegen output used unchanged as a tiling albedo. Reference: concepts/03-canyon.png.

Create a seamless square 2D albedo texture for the ground of the Inkstorm podracing game, using the attached canyon concept ONLY as an art-style and color reference. This is a flat orthographic top-down texture tile of compacted rust-orange sandstone dust and packed racing sand, evenly lit, filling every pixel edge to edge. Painterly animation art, hand-painted dry-brush marks, small broken jagged strokes, scattered worn fine pebble flecks, subtle wind erosion and smudged ochre/coral mineral variation. Warm burnt-sienna and ochre overall, selective dark plum-brown abrasion, muted apricot highlights. No directional light or cast shadows, no perspective, no horizon, no scene, no objects, no vehicle, no road borders, no text. Fine irregular mixed-scale detail across the whole image. Avoid thick parallel bands, floorboards, regular repeating lines and giant distinct stones. Tileable on both axes; consistent detail and value across borders. The intended in-game usage is a repeating ground surface, not a concept illustration.
