## Terrain coherence and regressions

B is preferable because the apparent road/basin junctions no longer dominate the descent as trenches or repeated steps. The road, sand shoulder, and basin now read more often as connected surfaces. The improvement is strongest at `launch-descent` and visible at `05-launch`; the approach is effectively unchanged.

The remaining long, nearly straight dark boundaries still read as surface joins rather than erosion. B reduces their depth more successfully than their geometric regularity. The left-hand pocket near the launch gantry remains abrupt, and several terrain areas retain broad smooth faces with weak transition detail.

The main tradeoff is reduced local height articulation. At the crest, the broader foreground shelf obscures a little of the downhill route. At the launch, smoothing the shoulders also reduces some near-ground contrast. These are smaller issues than A's obvious cuts, but the next revision should preserve a clear downhill profile while adding irregular, continuous slope changes rather than restoring the trenches.

## Strict concept parity

**FAIL.** Both candidates preserve an opening between large rock masses and a settlement landmark, but their landscape is much shallower in composition than the concept. A broad distant basin wall terminates the central view; the target instead layers numerous separated rock towers, intervening valleys, atmospheric depth, and a settlement distributed through the landscape. The candidates contain a few dominant, comparatively simple masses and large smooth ground areas. Their settlement reads as a compact industrial installation atop one mesa, rather than the target's extensive terraced complex.

The target's descent also feels embedded in a varied continuous landform, with foreground cliffs, overlapping midground shelves, dust, and distant formations all reinforcing scale. B's improved joins address a local artifact but leave these larger differences. **Retain B as the better terrain revision; do not mark the launch landscape concept-complete.**
