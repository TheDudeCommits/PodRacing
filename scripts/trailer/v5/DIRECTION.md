# V5 — THE LINE IS YOURS

## Intent

Make one race feel worth entering. Treat the four authored machines as the cast,
Inkstorm as a dangerous place, and passing a rival as the plot. The previous cut
copied timestamps without earning its escalation. This cut starts with anticipation,
introduces distinct silhouettes, launches into readable traffic, shows combat as
cause and consequence, catches its breath, then races to a clear invitation.

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2MtgIoToa7I
Reference research: its existing 50-scene Higgsfield analysis plus direct browser
inspection of its desert tracking and jungle POV compositions. Not a claim of
frame-by-frame audiovisual viewing. Reference footage and music are not reused.

## Capture changes

- Capture-only tracked cameras with subject selection, local offsets, lens control,
  moving eye and trackside eye. Terrain clearance keeps low cameras above ground.
- All eight staged racers use the four selectable authored pod bodies; earlier review
  scenarios accidentally showcased old procedural vehicle classes.
- Stage initial positions only, then run the game's actual 120 Hz physics/AI and input.
- Render two individual ticks per 60 fps frame so real dust/wakes/effects accumulate.
- Save lossless-in-time gameplay, telemetry and representative stills; encode directly
  to high-quality MP4 so no multi-gigabyte disposable JPEG sequences are necessary.
- Generated inserts are labelled in the EDL and disclosure, and remain outside public/.
