## Three highest-impact fixes, ranked

1. **Rebuild the landmark compositions and geological silhouettes before adding detail.** Give the salt straight a descending or sweeping sightline with angled slabs and smaller depth layers; stage the canyon arch as a heavy asymmetric bridge framing a luminous exit; place a dominant central rock mass between two clearly visible fork routes; make the launch crest reveal an actual valley and distant foundry silhouette; frame the finish with an elevated bend, barriers, and a visible gantry. Replace repeated column/rounded-stack silhouettes with a small authored family of broken mesas, blade formations, shelves, and talus bases. Vary their aspect ratios and orientations rather than only their size. Keep clear racing space, but let meaningful large forms fill the middle distance. **Acceptance:** at thumbnail scale and without HUD labels, the seven locations should have distinct identities and match their concepts' defining spatial beats.

2. **Unify terrain, architecture, and vehicle around painted material shapes and directional light.** Current rock strata are dense, evenly spaced horizontal lines; the road is granular; the surrounding sand has very broad flat polygon patches; metal is mostly clean color blocks. Use large painted warm/cool planes first, medium fracture and wear shapes second, and restrained fine marks last. Break strata with vertical fractures and irregular ledges; blend sand into rock through rubble, dust, and contact darkening; paint road marks along travel direction and soften the road edge. Give metal dark seams, exposed undersides, chipped edges, and local rust rather than uniform noise. Strengthen cool occluded canyon foregrounds and haze the distant forms so the eye reads near, middle, and far distances. Reserve intense cyan for engine interiors and small functional lights. **Acceptance:** surfaces should remain coherent when viewed at normal game size, with no one object looking photographic, flat plastic, or uniformly striped against the others.

3. **Replace dominant primitive structures and refine the foreground craft with a consistent mechanical kit.** The grid needs low hangar masses, asymmetrical fabric spans, clustered equipment, and a trussed start-light assembly. The foundry needs a few large elbow pipes and varied tanks connected by platforms, braces, and suspended machinery; repeated racks should support these landmarks. Add grounded bases where structures currently appear to float or meet sand abruptly, especially the foundry's right gate post. On the craft, build a wider layered shell, stronger fin silhouettes, exposed housings, believable joints, and flexible connection detail; recess and articulate the pilot instead of leaving a small angular figure perched above a broad empty cockpit. Preserve clear cyan engine reads, but give their rings depth and controlled luminous falloff. **Acceptance:** the vehicle and the first two industrial structures visible from a race camera should carry the target's authored, repaired-machine character without needing tiny decorative clutter.
