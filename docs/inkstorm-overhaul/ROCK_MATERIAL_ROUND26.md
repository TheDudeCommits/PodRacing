# Rock material iteration — round26

The first round26 material candidate is a substantial readability improvement over round25, according to the independent image-only review in `BLIND_ROCK_MATERIAL_ROUND26.md`. It is **not art-accepted**. The final combined round26 review and performance gates are now complete: the fresh world review remains0/7 art acceptance, while all four local adaptive full-race cadence gates pass. New round27 material experiments are separate candidates.

## Generated source and unchanged geometry

The built-in Codex imagegen tool generated a broad sandstone fracture map. The exact prompt and original output are retained in `assets/source/inkstorm/rock-mass-v2/prompt.md` and `rock-mass-v2.png`; the runtime copy is `public/assets/inkstorm/rock-mass-v2.png`. PNG size is 1,565,057 bytes and SHA256 is `63259db61df87a62d0770ec4399866dc2fb1e90b0f24d9d9c176da31c377a7d8`. It was copied without pixel editing. The original `rock-paint.png` is preserved.

The material changes do not displace vertices or change the physical field, course, cameras, colliders, placement lists, vehicle exports or terrain topology. Actual geological form remains a separate problem; material improvement cannot turn the two smooth launch masses into the target valley or make the original arch a convincing rock bridge.

The initial replacement retained three stone texture reads. The current medium-bed study uses six, with one additional shared mipmapped texture allocation and no additional geometry or draws. This cost is included in the exact completed round26 full-race freeze. Build and TypeScript passed for both captured candidates; shader compilation produced no browser errors in the actual scenes. The two capture harnesses retained 481 short ordinary live intervals at about60Hz (p95 16.7ms and16.8ms respectively). These are short capture-session observations, **not substitutes for a full uninterrupted race gate**. Round26's four complete racing phases are the latest full-race evidence.
