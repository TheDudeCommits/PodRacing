## Five highest-impact fixes

1. **Rebuild the missing section compositions before adding small detail.** In `04-fork`, make a monumental central buttress visibly separate a low left shelf and high right shelf, both readable from the approach. In `05-launch`, expose a real descending valley view and a distant industrial landmark rather than another rise beneath a green bar. In `07-finish`, create the banked cliff bend, retaining barrier, chevrons, and visible drop. For `01-grid`, replace the oversized blank arch as the main subject with a layered pit lane of low garages, canopies, work areas, and a dark start gantry. Keep `06-foundry` dense across several depths with linked pipework and grounded supports. **Visual pass condition:** with HUD labels hidden, each numbered section should be identifiable from its silhouette and spatial arrangement alone.

2. **Replace the repeated rock vocabulary with a hierarchy of authored landforms.** The open sections repeatedly show the same narrow, split-top towers and broad stepped mounds; the canyon walls have texture but lack convincing fractured mass. Establish a few unique large silhouettes per section—leaning salt-run slab, thick irregular arch, split-route buttress—then support them with medium terraces, broken shelves, sloped scree, and small rubble concentrated at bases. Vary rock orientation and fracture direction rather than merely changing scale. **Visual pass condition:** the near, middle, and distant rock layers should differ in shape, scale, and density; no repeated column profile should become the section's most memorable feature.

3. **Give the world a deliberate light structure and coherent surface response.** The concepts use golden directional light, deep violet/umber shade, atmospheric separation, and small cyan/amber accents. The actual open frames are broadly bright peach with weak separation between road, sand, and rocks, while Foundry metal reads unusually clean and pale. Establish large directional shadows that connect structures to terrain, darker recesses and contact at joins, warmer lit planes, cooler shaded planes, and softer/lower-contrast distant layers. Add material-specific wear: chipped paint and grime around metal seams, dusty lower surfaces, exposed rock edges. **Visual pass condition:** downscaled to a small thumbnail, the eye should follow a clear sequence of lit road, shaded framing mass, and distant focal point instead of one similarly bright field.

4. **Bring the hero craft and pilot up to the target's construction standard.** The current cylinders and turquoise cockpit occupy much of every image, so their simplicity remains constantly visible. Build a more sculpted blue/orange body with overlapping armor, recessed dark engine mechanisms, turbine depth, differentiated fins, flexible cable connections, and localized wear that follows exposed edges. Enclose the pilot deeper in the cockpit, with a credible helmet/shoulder silhouette and less rigid block-shaped arms; retain a visible driving posture. The target craft forms a coherent tapered machine, whereas the actual central body resembles a shallow open tub. **Visual pass condition:** a close garage view and the racing view should both read as a designed, assembled machine with a securely seated pilot, not colored primitive parts.

5. **Rework the road surface and route furniture as part of the landscape.** The broad uninterrupted brown stripes look like stretched wood grain and exaggerate every mound. Use varied-width wheel ruts and broken scuffs concentrated on racing lines, fine sand/gravel at appropriate scale, irregular shoulder blending, and scattered compacted patches. Replace the flat neon green sticks/bars with smaller cyan-lit fixtures whose housings and bases belong to the world; use barriers and signs where the route demands them. Make hazards visually identifiable in the world rather than relying on the central label—`SAND GEYSER` and `DUST INTERFERENCE` cards do not by themselves convey a grounded environmental event. **Visual pass condition:** the road remains clear without fluorescent stake dominance, and hazards can be identified from the scene while the next turn remains visible.

The next visual review should repeat the seven actual camera views, with matching aspect ratio and comparable vantage points to the concepts. This is not a request to reproduce every painted pixel: it is necessary to preserve the selected images' large forms, light hierarchy, material character, and section-specific spatial events. None of the scores above implies a runtime or handling verdict.
