Voice lines

The rivalry and final-lap voice lines are existing recordings from “Voiceover Pack: Fighter” by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). No voice was generated or synthesised; the browser copies were level-adjusted by −2 dB, given a 10 ms fade-in and encoded to Opus at 64 kbps.

  • “Loser” — played when you pass the racer who wrecked you.
  • “Prepare yourself” — played when a rival wrecks you and the grudge is marked.
  • “Combo breaker” — played when you wreck the racer who wrecked you.
  • “Final round” — played when you enter the final lap.

Race music is credited in race-set/CREDITS.html; the selection score and every sound effect in salt-dusk-v2/CREDITS.html.
