# Blockrunner shoulder chase — private candidate, 2026-09-08

Implementation-aware, not fresh blind. I individually viewed actual V9 `01-grid.png` and `live-drive.png`, plus concept12 during the preceding material review. The current chase shows a broad rear platform and relatively small pilot. The reference is useful for pilot/craft hierarchy, not as a geometric authority: its rear inset invents geometry and perspective.

The [private patch](blockrunner-chase-rig-v1.patch) supplies an explicit Blockrunner base rig: **11.3m boom, 7.9m eye height, 1.4m aim height**, relative to the camera subject/root. Existing speed contributions remain +0.8m boom/+0.5m height. Every other appearance receives `undefined`, preserving the exact old arithmetic. The original shared clearance 4.8 remains as the existing descent horizon compensation when the explicit rig overrides the base position. Route grade/lateral preview, junction framing, travel blending, springs, landing, shake, FOV, reduced motion and non-chase cameras are unchanged. There is no physics or source-geometry change.

This does not prove spring transients, external scenery clearance or arbitrary replay translations.
