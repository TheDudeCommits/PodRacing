## Visible local construction

Strengths:

- The principal span has an intelligible hierarchy: large pipe above a railed deck, repeated triangular truss bays, dark vertical members, attachment plates, and two cylindrical support legs.
- Pipe elbows visibly turn into connected runs. The approach and middle views show return loops, contrasting smaller service pipes, coupling bands, valve wheels, ladders, and intermediate access platforms.
- Broad collars, visible fasteners on some plates, foot braces, and small lamps give the bridge a useful industrial silhouette. These elements remain readable at the supplied gameplay framing.
- Rust-red vessels, dark structural steel, and pale service pipes form a coherent material/color vocabulary. Surface speckling provides some wear.

Unresolved visible issues:

1. **The lifting equipment is not mechanically legible.** Beneath the main span in `06-foundry` and `foundry-near-span`, the central orange element reads primarily as a square plate or small box with a tiny hanging link. Reference 11 clearly articulates a trolley, winding drum, pulley block, multiple suspended cables, and a substantial hook. That functional silhouette is not resolved in the supplied views.
2. **Connections are still dominated by smooth bands and slabs.** Main-pipe collars, deck bearing regions, and support connections lack the distinct paired flange lips, projecting bolts, saddle clamps, layered steel plates, and local edge highlights visible in reference 11. Some fasteners are present; their density and hierarchy are insufficient to carry the same construction reading.
3. **Several elbows retain visibly angular transitions.** Rounded routing is evident, but the pale return pipes in `foundry-middle` and some rust-red return loops show coarse silhouette changes and broad, simple bend surfaces. The reference distinguishes fabricated bend segments and their seams more clearly.
4. **Bases look simplified and detached from their surroundings.** The left portal foot has a readable brace and rectangular plinth, while nearby plant assemblies sit on broad, dark, nearly featureless platforms. They lack the reference's varied concrete footings, anchored plates, local rubble, and accumulated material around the contact edges. This is a visible integration issue, not a claim about unseen attachment geometry.
5. **Wear remains a relatively uniform surface treatment.** Large pipes and vessels are predominantly smooth muted orange surfaces with sparse dark mottling. Distinct chipped edges, concentrated grime around joints, worn steel faces, and material separation have not reached the reference's level of definition.

**Single highest-impact next construction edit:** replace the small central hanging plate with one clearly articulated lifting assembly: a visible drum on a bolted trolley, paired descending cables, a pulley block, and a substantial hook. Size and contrast it to remain recognizable in the same near-span framing. This would give the span its most conspicuously missing mechanical focal point; it would not by itself guarantee an 8.

## Full-scene parity

Strengths:

- The orange desert light, rust-red processing equipment, overhead pipe crossings, and canyon rock establish the intended foundry setting immediately.
- The main span frames a route through the facility, and the main/near-span views offer meaningful overhead and lateral enclosure.
- Multiple pipe sizes, catwalk elevations, tanks, and chimneys provide more than a single flat wall of scenery. Cyan equipment displays and small warm lamps introduce the target's accent colors.
- The route is visually readable, and the environment has a consistent stylized treatment.

Unresolved visible issues:

1. **The target's deep foundry canyon is missing.** Original 06 builds a long, narrowing view through repeated bridges, irregular tower clusters, pipe networks, rock shelves, and distant canyon walls. The candidate's main view terminates quickly at a broad dune and wall; approach, middle, and exit expose large areas of sky and open ground. The factory reads as repeated equipment groups beside a dune route rather than a continuous industrial canyon.
2. **Repeated modules and empty surfaces dominate.** Similar orange vessels, pale rectangular chimney shafts, long pipe runs, and dark rectangular platform edges recur with limited local variation. Wide bare banks, isolated rocks, and sparse track posts replace the target's broken rock, machinery, retaining structures, and densely occupied shoulder edges.
3. **The land and facility do not yet read as one authored place.** Large industrial platforms meet smooth dunes with abrupt dark boundaries; exposed factory groups are separated by broad open sand. Reference 06 interlocks rock strata, rubble, foundations, retaining walls, and plant architecture through the full depth of the scene.
4. **Lighting and materials have less depth and separation.** The candidate's orange/yellow sky, muted pipes, pale chimneys, and broad uniform shadows flatten major regions into a few large tones. The target has clearer hot sunlight, deep industrial recesses, lit metal edges, atmospheric separation, and brighter cyan fixtures that punctuate different distances.
5. **The visible density is uneven across the route.** The main span is the strongest authored moment. `foundry-approach` shows an isolated tower group next to an exposed cliff; `foundry-middle` becomes a repeated line of pipe loops and white shafts; `foundry-exit` is dominated by a dune, sparse gateway, and open sky. The atmosphere of original 06 is not sustained across these supplied views.

**Single highest-impact next scene edit:** compose a continuous canyon corridor around and beyond the main span, using irregular plant/rock sidewalls and several staggered overhead crossings that recede toward a narrow distant opening. The edit should replace the broad dune-and-sky gaps with a convincing layered vista from the existing views. Added microdetail on the current isolated modules cannot substitute for this composition change.
