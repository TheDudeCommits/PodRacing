# Teemto open cockpit V2 — tapered painted bodywork

Candidate: `teemto-open-cockpit-round31-v2-candidate.glb`.

SHA256: `af5d69514a51f7e9c089a1710e1587f46e053412b488268dc433f029576c0a7e`. Size: 7,698,824 bytes. Runtime/header geometry: 57,618 triangles, 4 body + 6 pilot = 10 opaque draws. The existing 60,000-triangle / 6-body / 6-pilot / 12-total limits remain unchanged. Every mesh definition is referenced.

V2 addresses the bounded cockpit-finish failure in `docs/inkstorm-overhaul/BLIND_FORK_COCKPIT_ROUND31.md`. V1's squared front and rear plates are replaced by closed tapered cowling: a low forward deck, rounded rear shoulder flowing into a narrow spine, and a flared side collar that joins the opening to the original lower body. The original Cabina atlas supplies the cobalt skins and ivory crown. A separate dark liner, narrow conforming service-panel seams, and twelve restrained fasteners establish the material and construction hierarchy. There are no new generated textures or alpha materials.

The supplementary construction reference is `docs/inkstorm-overhaul/concepts/09-open-cockpit-detail-round31.png`, SHA256 `880e17a9bc1f729816ead00df470a154ac66fe779e27aae6fb7986936b978f7b`. It supplements the original art targets; this source study does not claim parity with those targets.

The underlying former closure stubs are lowered inside the new cowling in this derivative: 414 cockpit-body vertices change height to stay beneath the finished skin. Pilot and engine positions, indices, UVs, normals, tangents, materials, textures, original node names, transforms, and hierarchy edges remain exact. No scale or camera adjustment occurs. The new rear skin extends to game z -7.39; the prior hull ended at -7.3335, a 5.7 cm increase in rear extent. All other preservation claims are established in the packaging and validation receipts.

The original imported source, V4C source/public, and V1 source/public files were independently rehashed after authoring and remain byte-exact.
