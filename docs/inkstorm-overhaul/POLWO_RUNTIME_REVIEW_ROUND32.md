## Prioritized revisions

1. **P1 — Make the complete vehicle readable from the actual chase view.** Evaluate the rear fin/shell occlusion, projected engine separation and visible pilot area together. Preserve the identifiable Polwo shape while finding a presentation in which the engine/tether/cockpit relationship and helmet-plus-shoulders remain distinct on an ordinary straight. A framing change alone may expose more detail, but it should be judged with road visibility; these screenshots do not justify declaring the source geometry wrong. Use the straight/live and angled fork views to check the result, rather than relying on a garage quarter view.
2. **P1 — Resolve the palette and construction at gameplay scale.** Give the main shell and engine panels deliberate orange/cobalt regions, readable metal rims/joints and controlled wear. Reduce the muddy broad-panel impression under the actual warm world light. Retain nozzle centering, but make the aft throat/rim depth read as engine construction rather than a bright circular badge. Add detail only where it remains visible at this chase scale; tiny texture noise will not fix this gap.
3. **P2 — Use the garage preview space to inspect the asset.** Increase useful model coverage and separation from the background while keeping both engines and cockpit within the card. Use a default angle that shows the pilot and major engine structure, with a restrained key/rim contribution so dark parts remain legible. Preserve the clear selection/launch hierarchy. Static appearance after this change still needs a separate interaction check.

Before claiming pilot-fit acceptance, supply a runtime view that actually exposes hands/control contact and retain a source cutaway for lower-body support. The present images establish neither a floating pelvis nor supported seat/pedal contact.
