Audio credits

Existing recordings and sound-design libraries. No new generated sound effects or music.

  • Rocket Boost Engine Loop — Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup), 2012. CC0 1.0. Public HQ preview transcoded with 3 dB headroom; used as propulsion.
  • Sci-Fi Sound Effects Library — Little Robot Sound Factory, 2015. CC BY 3.0. Medium spacecraft loop, Laser09 and Laser07. Original WAVs transcoded with 3–5 dB headroom; narrow playback-rate, gain, pan and filter changes in game.
  • Jet Engine Takeoff — dklon, 2013. CC BY 3.0. Existing jet/Doppler effect transcoded with 4 dB headroom; boost cue.
  • Heavy Metal Thud on Ground — 7of9Designs, June 2022. CC0 1.0. Public HQ preview transcoded with 2 dB headroom; collision cue.
  • 2 High Quality Explosions — Michel Baradari, apollo-music.de, 2009. CC BY 3.0. explode.wav transcoded with 4 dB headroom; rupture/mine cue.
  • Interface Sounds — Kenney, 2020. CC0 1.0. Unmodified click_001.ogg for restrained mechanical UI, pickup and countdown ticks.
  • Juggernaut — Scott Buckley, 2022. CC BY 4.0. Music by Scott Buckley — www.scottbuckley.com.au. Existing 1:00–2:15 excerpt with short edge fades, encoded 24 kHz/112 kbps; no rearrangement or generated stems.

The original “Now this is podracing” voice clip was supplied by the project owner. Its original bytes and playback rate are preserved; it is separate from the open-license sound library.
