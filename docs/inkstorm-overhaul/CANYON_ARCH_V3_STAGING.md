`scripts/blender/build-canyon-arch-v3.py` imports those volumes through Blender MCP into the separate **PodRacing — CC0 fractured canyon arch v3** scene. It composes unequal roots and shoulders, two basal shelf pieces, and a diagonally descending three-piece cap. Cap volumes turn the scan's long axis sideways. Final nonuniform scale ratios range **1.08–1.99**, avoiding flattened sheets. Every source triangle remains present; there is no remeshing, procedural displacement, regular bed function, or Boolean-generated blank shell. Only coincident import seams are welded and face orientation is recalculated. Actual scan relief, face assignments, and vertex colors remain the inputs; exact original normal bytes are not claimed after transforms.

The joined export has **one mesh, one primitive, one material, nine internally watertight components**, 37,800 triangles and 28,679 exported vertices. The exported geometry has zero degenerate triangles, inconsistent winding edges, boundary edges, and nonmanifold edges after welding coincident export seams. Khronos validation reports zero errors, warnings, infos, and hints.
