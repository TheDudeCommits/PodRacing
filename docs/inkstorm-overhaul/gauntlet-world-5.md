# Blind world art review — round 8

**Overall score against the painterly Inkstorm concepts: 4.0/10. Acceptance: FAIL.**

The captures establish a readable desert racer with a consistent warm palette and a recognizable twin-engine craft. They do not yet deliver the concepts' authored environments, machinery, scale, or painterly light. The difference is substantially missing composition and geometry, followed by lighting and material refinement. It is not a small texture-polish gap.

This review uses only the nine PNGs in `output/gauntlet/round-8` and the seven numbered concept PNGs in `docs/inkstorm-overhaul/concepts`. No source, prompts, receipts, or prior reports were inspected. The extra garage and live-drive images inform craft presentation only. These stills establish neither FPS nor physical traversal, collision, handling, animation, or runtime stability. A route absent from a screenshot is not proof that it is absent from the whole game.

## Five highest-impact gaps, in priority order

1. **Author the route-scale compositions for launch, fork, and finish. — Missing composition and geometry.** Place the camera and road so launch reveals a lower valley and distant industrial destination; make both fork branches legible around one major buttress; give finish a visible bank, outside drop, and directional barriers. These three images should be identifiable with filenames and HUD hidden. More texture detail cannot create these spatial events.

2. **Build convincing inhabited and industrial landmarks. — Missing geometry and set dressing.** At grid, replace the dominant plain doorway with a mechanical race structure embedded in curved pit bays, fabric canopies, and clustered service activity. At foundry, vary tank heights, add large bent pipe runs and overhead crossings, and bury or support foundations coherently. Establish a few large, asymmetric silhouettes before small bolts or wear. The goal is a place with an apparent purpose and scale, not simply a greater prop count.

3. **Replace the repeated rock-and-road kit with composed geology. — Primarily geometry, then material refinement.** Repeated twin-peaked towers, uniformly striated vertical walls, regular projecting shelves, and isolated small rock stacks are conspicuous across the set. Use fewer dominant shapes with different lean, width, fracture direction, and height; connect them through talus and varied shoulders. Preserve a readable road ahead of crests. Break texture scale and direction only after silhouettes and medium forms work.

4. **Make the foreground craft and pilot carry the target's mechanical weight. — Geometry, proportion, and integration.** The craft occupies a major fraction of every frame, so its simple pilot, flat braces, shallow-looking black cockpit, clean faceted nose, and limited engine silhouette detail cap the whole presentation. Recess the seated pilot into a shaped cockpit with a convincing helmet, shoulders, arms, and controls; add layered engine housings, projecting fins, exposed machinery, and articulated or flexible connections. Keep the existing readable twin-engine silhouette. Surface chips are secondary to these forms.

5. **Rebuild the value hierarchy and painterly material response. — Lighting first, texture refinement second.** The concepts use dark close masses, warm illuminated planes, cool recesses, and progressively lighter distant layers to guide attention. Current broad surfaces often sit at similar brightness, while ground, rock, machinery, and craft use visibly different levels of abstraction. Establish directional shadow bands, contact grounding, distant haze, restrained emissive bloom, and material-specific highlights. Then add broad painted variation and selective wear following object form; avoid relying on uniform striation or more micro-noise. Keep hazards readable without a large center-screen card becoming the strongest landmark.

## Acceptance boundary

Retain the coherent orange/cyan palette, recognizable vehicle silhouette, and the canyon/foundry section families. Do not sign off the Inkstorm target on that basis. Acceptance needs new game captures showing the missing spatial compositions and a more integrated foreground craft; matching colors and adding painterly noise would not resolve this review. Any performance or traversal acceptance must come from separate measured runtime evidence.
