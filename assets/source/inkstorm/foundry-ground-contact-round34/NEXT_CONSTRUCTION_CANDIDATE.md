Choose one bounded patch: **replace only the visual pipe-bank foundation boxes with closed, stepped concrete foundations whose exposed faces have real recessed joints and a lower edge seated into the existing terrain.** Keep the machinery, existing connections, source GLBs and all gameplay placement/collision data unchanged. This addresses the large smooth retaining-wall faces and abrupt sand contact visible in the actual near-span/middle frames. It does not promise to repair the route hidden by the crest.

The flange/recess finding needs an accurate source distinction. The current 19,960-triangle pipe bank already contains nine paired vessel bands, three recessed manways, gusseted decks and three flange/pressure-cap assemblies. Vessel contact shading/material separation can be judged separately once the new foundation is actually visible.

The proposed geometry is one repeated construction language, generated per placement once at course setup:

Use the current concrete color; exposed seam backs may use the existing graphite machinery color. No new lighting term or global palette change.

At the bottom, sample the same `heightAt` function at at most **2 m** intervals along exposed edges. Terminate the closed lower skin approximately **0.15 m below** those sampled heights, bounded by the existing bottom. Do not modify or flatten the terrain sampler. Where the terrain hides a joint, omit buried detail rather than raising it into view.
3. Break the perfect rectangular sand meeting with two or three small, attached footing fragments at existing corners, **inside the current footprint** and below **0.45 m above local terrain**. They must visibly contact both the main footing and sampled ground; no scattered roadside rocks or floating slabs. Clip or omit any proposed fragment that cannot meet those constraints. No new collision shape is added.
