The new scene is `PodRacing — Blockrunner geometry-aware Inkstorm paint round34 V4`. It contains two copied meshes, 44,028 triangles, the original V2 UVs and already encoded normals, unchanged transforms and material-index arrays, three body materials and four pilot materials. New procedural materials contain no copied V2 image targets. Source, fit, V2 and all preexisting scene memberships/context are checked after the call.

The body palette is cleaner warm ivory, richer oxide red and lighter graphite. Existing intake faces gain blade/hub value and roughness separation inside the oxblood material. Pilot roles remain identical: readable gray suit with geometry-based accents, gray/titanium helmet, dark visor with a restrained band and lower roughness, and pale warm gloves. All body materials keep identical non-color Principled defaults so one later body atlas material remains possible. The exact recipes are at the top of the author fragment.

The external analysis reads actual V2 indices from `paint-v2-wear-topology-receipt.json`. Source-object ranges and effective mirrored corner order come from the actual V2 author receipt. It uses a 1e-5 positional weld only in analysis arrays to detect real adjacency; no Blender vertices are welded. Coplanar diagonals, gentle curved facets below 30 degrees and edges shorter than 0.035 source units are excluded. Geometry and explicit contact zones select rim arcs, rail ends/studs, panel corners, disk arcs and a few cockpit margins. Intended holes remain intact. The front source components identify 160 existing blade faces and 1,120 existing hub faces; those identities affect color/roughness, not geometry.

Two new `FLOAT_COLOR`/`CORNER` attributes store edge distance and contact strength. Barycentric interpolation of opposite-edge altitudes gives a measured 0.055-source-unit band even on a large triangle; it does not spread an edge mask across an entire sparse panel. Medium-scale breakup modulates only selected bands. The conservative pre-breakup band-area bound is 2.90% of total body surface. Protected joins darken by at most 10%, using a warm gray color, never black. Pilot anatomy has no chipped-paint mask. Its 462 suit-accent faces are selected from existing suit geometry and large facing regions.

The receipt carries `paintSignature`, `paintCornerNormalSignature`, exact `paintAttributeSignatures`, `linkedPaintImages` (initially empty), mesh names, V2 `objectLineage`, and inherited `normalEncodingBudget`/`normalEncodingBudgetInherited`. The exact attribute guard helper is `paint_attribute_signatures(scene)` in `13-v4-attribute-signatures.py`. Bake/render/finalizer calls must preserve these actual value signatures, not only attribute metadata.
