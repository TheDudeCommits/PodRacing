# Blind foundry review — combined v4, round 33

Reviewed 2026-09-08. This is a fresh image-only verdict. I viewed each of the five submitted gameplay PNGs individually, then judged them against the original foundry concept and the supplemental construction reference, both also viewed individually. I did not read source, prior reviews, implementation notes, receipts, or other project documents before deciding the verdict. No browser, Blender, or runtime inspection was used.

The original `concepts/06-foundry.png` remains the scene-quality target. The supplemental `concepts/11-foundry-construction-round33.png` helps judge mechanical construction; it does not replace or lower the original scene target. Vehicle-design differences are not penalized. Natural cropping while passing beneath overhead structures is allowed and is not a defect in this review.

**Combined result: FAIL.** The foreground gantry is a credible industrial structure, and the facility is immediately recognizable. The remaining gap is visible construction finish across the whole installation and a substantial scene-composition gap against the original. Neither effort nor the existence of many individual parts earns acceptance by itself.

## Gate 1: local visible industrial construction

The strongest evidence is the main gantry in `06-foundry.png` and `foundry-near-span.png`. The orange upper pipe has collar segmentation, the span has repeated triangular bracing, the vertical members have visible joint plates, and the left column has a substantial plinth with diagonal foot braces. Circular service landings, handrails, small lamps, and the equipment cabinet at ground level make the assembly legible at the actual gameplay camera distance. The right column is partly buried by the sand bank; that burial is plausible and is not treated as missing support. The span reads as supported rather than as an isolated beam hanging over the road.

The tanks also have a useful hierarchy of bodies, caps, circumferential bands, access ladders, platforms, elbow pipes, flange rings, valves, and small control panels. In `foundry-approach.png`, the large right-side vessel has a particularly readable ladder-and-platform arrangement. Pipe runs frequently turn into vertical legs or visibly enter a vessel through collars. I do not see enough evidence to accuse the installation broadly of open or floating pipe ends.

Concrete defects keeping this gate below 8:

1. **The hoist remains a symbol at the main review camera.** Beneath the center-right of the gantry in `06-foundry.png`, the visible lifting unit reads chiefly as an orange rectangular block with a small dark loop. A load-bearing trolley, winch/drum body, cable path, and convincing hook are not clearly resolved as a connected mechanical assembly. The supplemental reference makes those elements readable as one machine even within the wider gantry view. This is a local landmark-quality shortfall, not a demand to duplicate its every bolt.
2. **Several long pipes lack a readable support relationship.** The thick blue overhead runs in `foundry-middle.png` and `foundry-exit.png` cross past the tall ivory uprights, but their contact areas do not clearly reveal cradles, straps, brackets, or a defined bearing surface. Tank-end collars are more readable than the intermediate support story. I am judging what the image communicates, not asserting hidden geometry is absent or that the structure is physically impossible.
3. **The broad pale cross-members around the main gantry obscure its otherwise clear assembly.** In `06-foundry.png` and `foundry-near-span.png`, the pale diagonal/horizontal members behind and below the truss read as thick plain slabs. Their junctions with the surrounding columns and platforms are much less articulated than the orange truss connections. They produce overlapping structural bands without an equally clear hierarchy of what supports what.
4. **The ivory towers are large but mechanically underdescribed.** In the approach, middle, and exit images, the repeated pale rectangular stacks with dark horizontal bands expose large plain shafts. They lack the useful endpoint, cap, duct, or apparatus silhouette that makes the orange vessels feel purposeful. Their scale gives this simplification considerable screen prominence.
5. **Surface finish is inconsistent with the amount of geometric detail.** Large tank bodies, pipes, and dark platform sides show broad color fields with sparse dark stains. Many flange and band edges have nearly the same unbroken appearance. There is limited visible differentiation between worn exposed edges, recessed joints, bases near sand, and the main sheet surfaces. This makes finished assemblies look simpler than their part count suggests.

The gantry itself is closer to acceptance than the full installation. An 8 for this gate would require its mechanical readability to extend to the pipe supports, pale structural members, and secondary towers visible in these same cameras.

## Gate 2: scene quality against the original concept

The submitted world retains the original's warm desert industry, orange pipework, cooler metal accents, huge vessels, and infrastructure spanning a racing route. The current gantry provides a strong overhead landmark. Warm ground light and cool shaded machinery create a usable palette, and the route is visually discoverable in the stills.

The original's strongest world qualities are not reproduced convincingly enough:

1. **The original is an inhabited industrial canyon; these views often read as equipment rows standing on open sand.** The approach image presents a relatively isolated vessel group beside a rock wall. The middle and exit views expose large sky and open desert gaps around repeated modules. In the original, vessels, secondary machinery, bridges, ledges, and canyon rock form overlapping masses from the near edges through the far distance.
2. **The foundry does not sustain a composed view into depth.** The original uses successive cross-spans and staggered towers to draw the eye along a long industrial corridor. In the main gameplay image, a broad sand mound and a relatively plain cliff close the central view shortly beyond the foreground gantry. The middle and exit cameras show more rows, but the repeated pipe-and-tank arrangement supplies less distinct near/middle/far hierarchy than the original.
3. **The ground occupies a large share of the composition without equivalent authored detail.** Broad, smooth dune forms dominate the lower and central scene. Sparse repeated rock stacks, route posts, and a few scattered stones do not deliver the original's broken shoulders, varied rubble, eroded ledges, and industrial debris. The issue is not that the road must be obstructed; its margins need richer shape and material transitions.
4. **Facility foundations feel placed on top of the terrain.** The long, dark, flat-sided plinths beneath the equipment in the approach, near-span, and middle views expose abrupt box-like edges. Some sand burial is present, but the platforms still frequently read as isolated display bases. The original merges masonry, metal supports, rock, and accumulated debris more convincingly.
5. **Repetition is easier to notice than functional variation.** Tank caps, banded shafts, pale towers, elbow loops, small cyan panels, and railing strips recur with very similar proportions and treatment. The original has more variation in vessel diameter, silhouette, bridge elevation, attached machinery, and irregular secondary detail. Its industrial density therefore reads as a place with different processes rather than mainly repeated modules.
6. **Light and material depth remain too even.** The current sunset is attractive, but the original has a stronger progression of luminous distance, shaded foreground masses, lit edges, warm practical lamps, and localized cyan accents. The gameplay facility has small lamp/panel details, yet they seldom establish an equally persuasive focal hierarchy. Broad, relatively flat tank and ground shading reduces the sense of immense machinery surrounding the route.

These are world-quality observations. I am not lowering the score because the vehicle is smaller, differently designed, or seen from a different chase camera. I also do not count the top-edge crop of pipes and gantry members during passage as a composition failure.

## What can and cannot be called an improvement

No earlier gameplay images were supplied to this blind review. I cannot establish a before/after improvement or attribute any visible strength to this iteration. The current gantry's braces, footings, plate joints, rails, collars, lamps, and service details are convincing present-tense strengths. They are evidence of construction readability, not proof that a previous defect was fixed.

These stills establish no FPS, frame pacing, continuous motion quality, collision behavior, clearance during driving, control quality, or runtime stability. The HUD speed does not verify motion performance. No such claims contribute to either score.
