# Salt Dusk audio replacement

Existing human-authored music, field recordings and catalogue effects replace the runtime oscillators, generated noise and procedural score. The user explicitly prohibited generating new sound effects or music. No audio generation service, synthesized fallback, sample-writing function, procedural score, or runtime oscillator remains active in `src/audio`. Existing files are decoded and played with gain, filtering, panning and playback-rate changes only. Removed synthesis source/tests are archived in `assets/source/audio-salt-dusk/withdrawn-synthesis/`; they are not imported or executed.

## Preserved clip and runtime behavior

`public/audio/podracing-selection-intro.webm` is byte-identical: SHA256 `39c4d411570a2591be5cbf7fc27d86a63c36d3befa5de8d57c0d2018e1c89260`. Selection playback, menu-to-race mix transition, cached overtake playback, anti-spam cooldown and ducking are preserved. This user-supplied clip remains separate from the open-license bank.

The 27 runtime files total **2,213,998 bytes**. Kenney files are unchanged copies. Engine has 3dB headroom and Opus encoding; fan and wind are trimmed, level-adjusted and edge-faded. Juggernaut uses the existing 75-second excerpt, short edge fades and 24kHz/112kbps encoding, without rearrangement or generated stems. It repeats as an excerpt, rather than claiming a newly composed seamless score. Browser Web Audio resamples decoded data to its context rate: the entire bank plus supplied intro occupies **48,863,208 decoded bytes at 48kHz** in the native probe, including the 28.8MB music buffer. No full 222-second music buffer is decoded at runtime.

Eight continuous buffer-source voices cover engines, turbine, wind and four rivals. Transient combat playback is capped at 24 voices; redundant same-kind contacts are rate-limited. Paused events are discarded. Each finished/stolen transient disconnects its source and gain. Disposal stops all sources, disconnects nodes, clears decoded/pending caches and closes the context. Unavailable files remain silent and appear in the read-only `recordingStatus`; no stale sounds are queued and no synthesized fallback is used. Simulation, HUD and renderer ownership are unchanged.

The independent lifecycle review found and fixed two menu/background defects. Returning to selection now cancels all vehicle gain automation, zeros the eight pooled vehicle voices and canyon return, clears combat/callout sources and discards old telemetry. Intro and music continue through the same sources. Menu mix changes and late telemetry cannot restart those engines; a race transition plus fresh telemetry activates them again. `setHidden(boolean)` immediately mutes the master independently of user pause/mute/music settings and clears gameplay transients. Source clocks continue silently rather than racing asynchronous context suspend/resume calls; becoming visible restores the current user mix, including pause or mute. Hidden events are discarded, and returning to an unpaused garage while hidden remains silent.
