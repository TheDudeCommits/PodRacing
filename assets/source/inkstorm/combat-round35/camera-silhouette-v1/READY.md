# Combat camera silhouette candidate — implemented, awaiting native review

The V7 stills show a narrow end-on wreck and a gate beam over its cockpit. Source inspection found the concrete mismatch: the imported body rotates using `stateYaw + presentationTime * 1.25`, but the camera used only authoritative yaw. Moving the V7 camera farther away preserved this collapsed silhouette. Actual V7 entry/hold/return PNGs and the fresh critic were inspected; this implementation review is not blind.

Changed only `CinematicCamera.ts`, camera subject/cut framing in `GameApp.ts`, and `CinematicCamera.test.ts`. The camera patch excludes the concurrently owned `consumeGalacticEffects` changes. Simulation, wreck pose, source art, effects, course, collision, replay offsets and ordinary driving rigs remain under their existing owners.

- At cut entry, measure the active imported mesh bounds in racer-local space using their real transforms. No source geometry or bounding-box metadata is changed. Hidden procedural bodies and VFX are excluded. A bounded procedural fallback remains available.
- Each cut frame, transform that center with the exact displayed rigid wreck yaw/pitch/roll. Aim at this center from lateral 28/back 28/up 14 instead of the old near end-on right 12/back 48 rig. A measured radius accommodates larger assets; portrait clearance retains its screen-aspect adjustment. Camera vectors never become simulation inputs.
- After the cut, restore the selected camera mode. Only chase after a real preceding cut receives bounds clearance while the same wreck remains active. The exit springs toward the ordinary driving heading; a sphere-fit radial guard prevents the spring chord passing through the craft. The aim stays on its displayed center. Recovery removes this temporary target and the normal spring returns toward ordinary chase. Manual cockpit/side views and reduced-motion paths with no cut do not receive this treatment. Pause/capture/nonracing state clears it.
