**Five ranked corrections**

1. **Simplify the HUD’s secondary layer.** Consolidate over-rev and heat into one warning treatment; reduce persistent impact/route labels and surrounding boxes. Preserve the curved speed instrument while separating boost, heat, and damage more clearly. The lower-center impact badge occupies the forward play area; the upper-left warning obscures additional scenery.

2. **Establish a stronger type hierarchy across menus.** Reserve stencil lettering for major titles, enlarge section headings and essential values, and reduce tiny uppercase annotations. The references use broader, heavier typography with more decisive scale changes.

3. **Rebalance Build around comparison.** Enlarge alternative-part names, make equip actions visibly actionable, and use the empty lower space to spread modifiers into aligned rows. Strengthen the distinction between installed equipment, available choices, and aggregate results.

4. **Give the map more deliberate graphic structure.** Use fewer, stronger orbital strokes and a clearer ordering of event nodes. Increase label contrast and reinforce selection beyond planet size. Add restrained surface texture and larger graphic accents without copying reference symbols.

5. **Clarify the garage’s selection sequence.** Visually subordinate body variants beneath vehicle class, increase separation between machine selection and event selection, and simplify the footer around Start Race. Give stat names and values more room.

The candidates capture the references’ **palette and recognizable shapes** more convincingly than their typographic confidence, spacious hierarchy, and visual restraint.
