**Three remaining priorities**

1. **Deepen localized contacts:** strengthen shadows beneath studs, between assembled pieces, and around the seat; avoid uniformly thick outlines.
2. **Differentiate surfaces:** add restrained edge chips, recessed grime, and roughness variation to red paint, cream panels, and dark rails.
3. **Separate the pilot:** distinguish suit, seat, and cockpit values; improve helmet/shoulder lighting so the seated figure remains readable in both sunlight and shade.
