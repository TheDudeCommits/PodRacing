## Executed surface changes

- **Engine front bands:** continuous world-Y interval −5.805 to −5.18, restricted to 1,280 existing ivory shell faces resolved from Ivory's own engine components. Original fans, open intake/throat, hub and rear metallic roles remain exact. This is a shader marking on existing faces, not a new ring or cut.
- **Rear cockpit stripe:** world-X interval 0.57–0.88, allowed only on the two identified rear slope objects (640 eligible ivory faces). The visible positive-X slope receives the stripe, including its existing stud surfaces. These counts describe eligible masks, not the count of wholly orange triangles.
- **Original pilot shoulder accents:** only the exact own 2,508-face slate suit role can receive orange, further bounded by distance from source center X 0.053 >0.315, Z 2.52–2.77 and Y <2.40. Original pilot geometry, hands, fitted controls and seat remain unchanged. The common mesh still contains its correct **700 body + 4,128 pilot** faces.
- **Visor:** a narrow upper amber color band and tiny bounded front-lens gleam on the existing 200 visor faces. This is an authored fixed highlight intended for later base-color baking, not a new reflection/light source.
- **Edge wear:** two new CORNER FLOAT_COLOR attributes carry marking authority and barycentrically interpolated distance to qualifying actual edges. Coordinate keys rounded to six decimals are used only for analysis; no Blender vertices are welded. A manifold two-face, convex, source-normal-angle test identifies 14,979 analysis edges across the 53 meshes. Triangle diagonals that do not meet that test are excluded. Edge widths are 0.005–0.019 source-world units, with a zero-including continuous position-noise breakup (scale 18), restrained role-dependent strengths and a 0.035 maximum broad patina mix. This avoids face-random color assignment. The classifier is a bounded wear guide, not a complete semantic labeling of every physical rim.
- **Contact tint/roughness:** a short 0.12-unit AO distance, 16 samples, `only_local=false`, contributes at most 0.12 contact-dust pigment (0.025 on suit/visor/gloves); roughness varies modestly around each inherited role value. No normal input is connected or rewritten. No directional light shadow was baked.

All inherited source/role FACE values, material indices, ordered source polygon corners, vertices/edges, smooth flags, UV values, existing attributes, raw custom corner normals, hierarchy and local/world transforms remain exact. The nine material slots retain the previous role histogram; orange markings are additional shader masks, so the histogram must not be misreported as final visible pigment area. No Colour variant polygon data was transplanted. The new masks and complete relevant node properties/defaults/links have separate exact signatures in the author receipt and both render guards.

## Actual image review and gap

Both actual PNGs were viewed individually, then compared with both original palette views and concept 21. They reuse the exact palette camera transforms/orthographic scales, 768×576 resolution, CPU Cycles 16 samples/seed 3401, neutral world and two white area lights, Standard/None exposure 0/gamma 1.

The engine bands, rear stripe and shoulder patches now read clearly. The original pilot, open intake/fan depth, fitted controls and vehicle silhouette remain recognizable. However, **the material finish remains much cleaner and flatter than concept 21**: edge wear and contact dust are subtle at these views, the visor is mostly dark, and the target's richer rough-surface contrast and worn chips are not yet matched. The fullcraft view also gives less screen area to the model than the generated target; the matched camera was retained for a fair palette comparison. No score or final art pass is claimed.

## Deferred pipeline

This stage is **source-only, artAccepted=false, runtimeReady=false**. No UV change, texture/atlas bake, consolidation, normalization, export, public copy, registry/fleet admission or core edit occurred. It does not add a fifth registered family.

If the finish is later accepted for a bake trial, work on another copy: separate the common 700 body faces from its 4,128 pilot faces; retain original polygon/corner-normal lineage and effective mirrored parity while consolidating body/pilot surfaces; make UVs only on those copies; explicitly bake the final `Ivory Finish Color` and `Ivory Finish Roughness` outputs with both owners present for contact AO. Final atlas materials should clamp rather than repeat, preserve response roles, and remove the two temporary color attributes only from a separately validated export copy after baking, avoiding unintended `COLOR_0` tint. No part of that deferred work ran here.
