## Root integration

`runtime-material.patch` adds an optional third constructor argument to the existing material. When supplied, its baked variant replaces analytic diffuse arithmetic with one RGB lookup. It retains the source-local warm lens mask, current cyan predicate, wear, sun lighting and haze. With no third argument, the existing analytic path remains available for A/B and rollback. The staged shader compiled in the GPU probe; no runtime source was edited by this agent.

Load the paired UV GLB and PNG before creating the baked pit material. Configure the texture with `colorSpace=SRGBColorSpace`, `flipY=false`, clamp wrapping, and normal mip filtering. Construct `new InkstormSurfaceMaterial(false, 'pit-complex', { texture, decodeRange: 4 })`. Do not apply a second color conversion, normal term, sun-visibility factor, AO term or extra division byπ. Existing worn base color multiplies the decoded white response once. Validate that the clone/merge path retains `uv1`; fail visibly if a mismatched or missing map/geometry pair is loaded.

The district branch in the staged patch assumes two horizontal scale tiles and was compile-tested only. **Do not supply a baked district texture:** none exists. Its optional CPU UV trial narrowly failed7.899 pixels against the8-pixel padding requirement, so it was not transferred or baked. All district work is paused until the pit image comparison is graded. Separate terrain/foundations and moving racers are outside the family bake and neither receive it nor cast into it.

## Source provenance and execution limits

The final CPU UV producer is `uv-xatlas.mjs`, with pinned MIT `watlas@1.0.1` tools under this staging directory. It copies source attributes and uses source IDs; `validate-uv.mjs` and `transfer-uv1.mjs` verify the actual exported triangles and final per-corner equality. The earlier Blender SmartProject layouts failed the strict overlap/density tests and are diagnostic history, not final inputs. One failed post-edit UV adjustment used a stale Blender RNA reference and corrupted temporary source-ID data; it was rejected by the validator and replaced before any final bake. Original source data was unaffected.

Two full pit128-sample bakes were performed: the first unweighted square pilot, then the denser priority4 atlas delivered here. The first EXR is preserved in `versions/first-square-pilot/`; only the priority4 bake is paired with the candidate GLB. Calibration bakes are separate tiny diagnostic images. No district bake, camera beauty render, full `.blend` save, source/public mutation or credential access occurred. All94 pre-existing scene memberships, Going Merry scene, ViewLayer, Sketchfab_model.001 active object, and15-object selection were restored and asserted after Blender mutations.

Actual-game A/B, material readability, distant atlas bleed, renderer resource counts and frame timing remain root's pending checks.
