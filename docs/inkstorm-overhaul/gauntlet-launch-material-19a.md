## Five highest-priority visible fixes

1. **Build a connected launch basin with a stronger crest reveal.** Let the crest composition expose a substantial valley floor and distant route below. Connect the large rocks through shelves, eroded slopes, talus, and secondary formations so their bases do not appear to sit independently on a smooth sand plane.
2. **Create distinct near, middle, and far terrain layers.** Add a framing cliff or substantial foreground shoulder, vary the silhouettes and scales of the middle-distance formations, and carry lower-contrast terrain farther toward the horizon. Reduce the wide empty sky and uninterrupted sand bands in the approach/crest compositions.
3. **Embed the distant factory into the landscape.** Expand its footprint along the rock shelf with visible foundations, service terraces, connecting pipe runs, and smaller structures. Give the primary silhouette enough scale and layered complexity to register as a canyon landmark rather than a few distant tanks.
4. **Make foundry surface treatment structural and material-specific.** Preserve the readable broad shapes, then add larger paint wear, seam dirt, ring-joint grime, selective exposed edges, and differentiated light response on tanks, elbows, bands, and platforms. The current fine mottling is **too subtle and too evenly distributed**, not an overwhelming noise problem. The launch rocks have the opposite local issue: their similarly crumpled surface detail competes with their broad forms. More uniform noise will not resolve either gap.
5. **Expose the garage calendar's extent and keep complete entries visible.** Provide a clearer scrollbar or remaining-event cue, avoid cutting the Foundry description mid-line, and make the event referenced by the workshop guidance easier to find. The vehicle/layout hierarchy already works; prioritize the list and its relationship to the event rules.
