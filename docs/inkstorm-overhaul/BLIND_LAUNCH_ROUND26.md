## Three highest-impact fixes

1. **Make the crest reveal a deep descending canyon and an unmistakable landing corridor.** Break or lower the continuous central mesa barrier so the view contains a near ledge, a clearly lower landing, two or three staggered valley formations, and a distant settlement layer. Place the tallest mass off center; use the road's broad light-and-shadow shape to carry the eye down and left, with markers supporting that shape. In the approach, crest, and launch frames, the launch edge and destination should be distinguishable without reading the HUD. The crest should visibly reveal more depth than the approach rather than another broad shelf before large walls.

2. **Give the canyon one coherent sculpted and painted rock language, including its contact with the sand.** Replace the dominant rounded, vertically draped wall silhouettes with stepped strata, broken shelves, undercuts, and unequal towers at large and medium scales. Reduce the jump from smooth mesas to finely jagged accessory spires. Carry ochre ledges, violet shadow planes, and restrained painted edge accents consistently across both. Merge cliff feet and isolated rocks into sloping talus, partly buried fragments, and wind-driven sediment bands; retain an open driving line. Success means the smaller formations appear to have eroded from the larger ones, and rocks no longer read as objects set on a sandy floor.

3. **Raise the foreground vehicle to the same authored visual standard as the environment.** Its central placement makes the simplified engine shells and long narrow cockpit impossible to overlook. Add a clear hierarchy of turbine rings, armored shell breaks, exposed mechanical recesses, and distinctive fins; articulate the cyan exhaust as a recessed luminous mechanism rather than a flat pale disc. Give the cockpit a readable seated-pilot silhouette and more intentional massing. Use larger controlled blue/orange paint shapes and localized edge wear to describe those forms. Judge this at the actual gameplay framing: the vehicle must read as a powerful assembled machine before any fine surface detail is considered.

The palette and the industrial landmark establish a useful direction, but these screenshots do not demonstrate parity with the concept or passage of the strict visual gate.
