# Combat breakup target — round 35

This is a generated art-direction target, not a native gameplay screenshot, a runtime asset, or evidence that crash rendering is implemented.

The selected built-in imagegen result shows two detached engine bodies on asymmetric trajectories with visible breaks in both tow linkages. Concentrated white/amber fire, charcoal smoke, small fragments and low grounded dust preserve readable equipment silhouettes. Muted blue enamel, cream panels, orange repairs, painted sandstone and restrained ink accents retain the established Inkstorm palette.

Use the image to guide the relative scale of the main wreck bodies, exposed connections, short flame source, smoke lobes and dust contacts. Generated mechanical detail and environment architecture are illustrative and require separate production decisions. The concept does not establish fracture physics, collision correctness, performance or visual acceptance of the running game.

One new scene was generated and then revised once to make both linkages visibly severed and simplify the shading. The final native PNG was copied into this directory; both original generated outputs remain at their original locations. Existing concept images were not replaced.

Exact initial and revision prompts, inspected references, source paths, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `15-combat-breakup-round35.json`.
