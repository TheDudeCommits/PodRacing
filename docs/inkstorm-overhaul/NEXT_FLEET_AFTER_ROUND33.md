## Measurements before export

The saved `catalogue-inspection-round32/<uid>-receipt.json` records source-scene bounds in the inspection frame. Derived dimensions are approximately **5.868856 wide × 12 long × 3.153472 high** for both. Colour bounds are `[-2.909946,-28.763988,0.021513]` to `[2.958909,-16.763992,3.174984]`; ivory bounds are `[-2.934428,-6.206345,0.021513]` to `[2.934427,5.793655,3.174984]`. Their centres differ substantially: colour `[0.024482,-22.763990,1.598248]`; ivory `[-0.000001,-0.206345,1.598249]`. These are bounding centres, not measured seats or accepted gameplay pivots.

1. In a new isolated derivative scene, map original objects/materials to cockpit, left/right engine, physical crossbeam and source pilot. Record transforms and instance-expanded triangles. Bake rigid meshes: `VehicleArtLibrary` rejects skinning, instancing and morphs, and the presentation adapter requires opaque single-material primitives with positions/normals.
2. Measure source forward/up from actual geometry and engine openings. The views suggest Blender **-Y forward / +Z up**; this is an inference to confirm. Existing exports use glTF **+Z forward / +Y up**. Preserve a transform receipt for each UID; the colour source's large Y offset must not survive into the runtime root.
3. Choose a uniform scale and translation against the existing normalized podracer envelope, seat datum and collision/probe layout. No exact scale is accepted here. Do not use whole-model centre as the seat, copy Teemto's scale, or change physics to disguise a mismatch. Measure driver pelvis, head/eye height, hand/control contact, cockpit floor/rim, underside and left/right extents. Keep the established `podracer` class, handling, collision radius and records.
4. Author and record model-root `pilot`, `exhaustLeft`, `exhaustRight`, `couplingLeft`, `couplingRight` coordinates. Measure the **rear opening centres, radii and axes**, not intake fans or engine bounding-box tips. Hero/rival exports must retain identical anchor coordinates and normalized basis. Existing wake/dust/effect code reads attachment positions directly, so root-space anchors are the safest current contract.
5. The source already has engine throat openings and a solid front crossbeam. Decide from the measured derivative whether `hasAuthoredExhaustHardware: true` is appropriate; do not add a duplicate nozzle lip. Coupling anchors must place any energy effect clear of the crossbeam. If the solid connector needs no energy effect, intentionally omit the two coupling attachments (the current renderer only creates the coupling when both exist) and document that presentation choice.
6. Consolidate body to **at most six draws**, pilot to **at most six**, total **at most twelve**. Target fewer by merging equivalent opaque surfaces while retaining driver/body separation. Hero must be **≤60,000 instance-expanded triangles**; rival **≤30,000**. Keep pilot shape/pose intact while reducing hidden/internal body surfaces and small distant studs for rival LOD. Do not raise adapter limits to admit unprepared sources.
