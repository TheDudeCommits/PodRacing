# Teemto rigid engine breakup — implementation checkpoint

Implemented on 2026-09-09 after the V10 temporal critique. This is a renderer-only candidate awaiting root's combined build, native capture and cadence checks. It does not have visual acceptance.

The actual eight admitted hero/rival GLBs were inspected without loading a browser. `actual-source-structure.json` pins their bytes, hashes, hierarchy, referenced triangle counts and bounds. Teemto has independent `teemto-engine-left.001` / `teemto-engine-right.001` groups, each containing its corresponding `*-body` mesh. Both packages use identity local transforms. Hero remains 57,618 triangles/10 meshes; rival remains 26,180/7. Sebulba has two material-consolidated body meshes; Polwo and Blockrunner each combine their engines in a main body mesh. Those families retain the existing rigid wreck presentation. No runtime GLB, texture or public asset changed.

`TeemtoWreckBreakup` prepares two engine support caches and the stationary cockpit/pilot bounds when the imported lease is installed. It transforms the two existing instance-owned groups around measured engine centres. Geometry, indices, normals, UVs, materials and pilot/cockpit transforms remain unchanged; no additional meshes, geometry uploads, programs or draw submissions are introduced. Source templates and other racer leases remain independent. Existing identity child MRT proxies follow the engine matrices; existing player-shadow proxies already copy their beauty source's current world matrix.

Motion is a direct function of the current wreck timer plus optional fractional tick: outward 3.2/4.2 m, longitudinal +1.3/−1.7 m, yaw −.26/+.38 rad, pitch +.13/−.20 rad and roll −.22/+.22 rad. It reaches its separation envelope over .26 simulation seconds and returns smoothly during the final .35 seconds. Age zero, ordinary/garage/replay updates, recovery and lease release restore exact original local transforms. No state, network packet, timer or authoritative collision is modified. This is a recoverable presentation of separated components, not a fracture simulation.

The hero's cockpit mesh includes two forward connector components (144 triangles each in the current GLB, verified by exact-position welding on an external analysis structure). They remain with the cockpit as loose tether ends; they are not guessed, sliced or hidden. The intact procedural flame, energy coupling and nozzle overlays are hidden during separation. Rupture anchors use the same current resolved engine matrices before applying the root wreck pose; authored anchor groups remain unchanged.

Each engine receives a positive world-Y correction from at most 74 cached real source samples. This adds at most 148 terrain queries per resolve, on top of the existing rigid support solver. No source vertices are read on a cache hit. Camera bounds are formed from the stationary source box and the two transformed engine boxes in assembly coordinates, then converted to a world-space centre/radius. This avoids needless world-axis box inflation. Existing camera mode, comfort and replay logic remain unchanged.

Actual CPU validation: 5 breakup tests plus 99 adapter/lifecycle/rigid/camera/FX tests pass (7 files total); typecheck and diff check pass. At 26 native trajectory checkpoints per package, all tested POSITION vertices cleared terrain and remained inside the camera sphere: hero minimum .550 m, maximum radius 21.353 m, maximum 118 extra support queries; rival .533 m, 21.331 m and 114. Rival is a geometric substitution on the recorded Teemto route, not a separate driving run. Tests also verify source/index preservation, lease isolation, seated pilot/cockpit stability, MRT following, exact reset, current-tick anchor ordering and absence of update-time geometry reads.

The measured support approximation is not a continuous triangle collider or proof of clearance on every terrain/bridge location. CPU checks do not establish 40–60 FPS or a good impact shot. Actual capture must judge the newly exposed connector ends, engine separation, settled contact, return/reassembly and the widened framing. Existing generic ground dust/contact blob placement remains based on the authoritative vehicle footprint; this candidate only synchronizes the rupture anchor and solid engine/shadow transforms. No extra debris pool was added.

`source-freeze-inventory.json` contains exact source/evidence hashes, validation scope and the two corrected test-fixture failures. `teemto-tests-final.log` and `integration-tests-final.log` are the actual final targeted outputs. Root's combined verification and visual/cadence evidence remain pending.
