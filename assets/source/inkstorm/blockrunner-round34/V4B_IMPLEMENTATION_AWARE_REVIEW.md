# V4B actual source-paint review

2026-09-08. **Implementation-aware review, not a fresh blind review.** This reviewer prepared the masks and author fragments, then individually opened all four actual V4B PNGs and concept 12. No new numerical score or acceptance gate is assigned. The neutral source views do not establish runtime or full-scene quality.

The pilot and intake treatment are materially clearer than V2. In the driver view the gray/titanium helmet, dark visor band, gray suit, pale hands and held grips remain distinct. Arm and torso forms no longer collapse into the same dark block. Front and fullcraft views retain that improved pilot read at smaller scale, while the separated fan blades and hubs make the engine mouths easier to understand. The ivory, graphite and oxide-red families remain coherent. The source silhouette, open cockpit, long rails, physical crossbeam and actual rear exhaust openings remain recognizable.

V4B also improves the specific wear problem visible in V4. In the driver view, near-panel stud rims have missing sections instead of complete pale washer-like rings. Front/fullcraft views show gaps in the engine-edge marks and in several rail-stud arcs. Rear wear is sparse and asymmetric. It is now visibly localized, although it still follows repeated part edges strongly.

The remaining differences from the transferable parts of concept 12 are:

- **Wear still has a limited shape vocabulary.** Several engine marks read as soft gray dabs along a seam, and groups of rail studs retain similar pale arcs. The target has a richer mix of chipped boundaries, directional abrasions and varied exposed undercoat. The zero-including breakup helps; it does not by itself create that variety.
- **Large ivory surfaces remain mostly clean.** The front cockpit slope, upper rear slopes and disk centers show much less abrasion than the concept. Their edge-local treatment preserves clear forms, but the target's wear extends onto some high-contact faces. This is an appearance gap, not evidence that source geometry should change.
- **The pilot finish is readable but simple.** The visor band reads as a clear graphic cue and the helmet now separates from the ivory body. Their balance under final game lighting and distance still needs observation; the neutral source still cannot establish glass response or dynamic reflections.
- **Texture conversion remains a separate check.** These four images are the procedural V4B source views. Four successful bake receipts prove execution and preservation, not the appearance of final downsampled textures. Check the final atlas copy for softened chips and localized dark seams without changing the preserved UVs speculatively.

No penalty is assigned for missing hangar lighting, cinematic staging, the concept inset's invented rear engine assembly, or different generated pilot proportions. Concept 12 remains a paint/material reference; the original source and accepted fit remain geometry authority.
