The fixed simulation tick remains 1/120 s. The existing mastery observer invalidates a wreck before its solo cinematic is admitted; host/guest scheduling stays real time. Pause, explicit camera commands, menu/capture changes and comfort settings cancel or suppress the camera treatment as appropriate.
- Two original Open Expedition pickups: **EMP Cell**, which interrupts nearby enemy boost/weapons and clears hostile ordnance, and **Repair Salvage**, which repairs actual hull damage and vents vehicle/core heat. Shields, allies, recovery immunity and clean-event exclusions are respected. V10 changes these two pickups to return after five simulation seconds for other racers, with one claim of each pickup per racer per race. The six older upgrade pickups retain their existing behavior.
- Local synthesized EMP, shield-impact and repair sounds using the existing audio context and pools. Critical listening/mix acceptance remains open.
