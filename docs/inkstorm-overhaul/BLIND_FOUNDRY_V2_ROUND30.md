## Top three remaining defects

1. **Unresolved network joins and support logic.** Resolve the overlapping cream walkway/gantry junction and clarify pipe terminals, bends, hangers, brackets, and span supports. Added infrastructure should read as assembled construction at each meeting point.
2. **Insufficient continuing corridor depth.** The paired view ends at a mound and canyon wall; the extras expose large open gaps and a bare exit horizon. Carry distinct bridge, pipe, vessel, and canyon layers into the distance while preserving a visible route opening beneath them.
3. **Repeated coarse equipment lacks the target's scale and material richness.** Repeated tank bodies, broad surfaces, thin catwalks, and sparse small parts still dominate. Vary vessel proportions and add readable service platforms, structural bases, machinery, conduits, purposeful blue lighting, and weathering that follows construction. More large pipes alone will not close this gap.
